## Question

How should the site position you now that you are a full-time AI Engineer at GDP Labs: hero line, subline, the primary call-to-action (email, CV, projects), whether the employer is named, how the About blurb reads, and whether the headshot (`images/image_1.JPEG`) appears in the hero.

## Resolution

Position as an AI Engineer only, in plain sentence-led copy with no typing animation and no job-seeking language.

- **Hero line**: plain identity line, "I'm Rama Ranuh, an AI Engineer."
- **Subline**: one sentence of scope (what you build/ship with LLM applications). Availability is left out. Exact wording is drafted at build time.
- **Employer**: GDP Labs is named only in the resume timeline (ticket: Resume section and CV handling), not in the hero or subline.
- **Primary CTA**: email (`mailto:`). Secondary: a text link to the CV and a GitHub/LinkedIn row. "See projects" is just scroll, no button.
- **Headshot**: `images/image_1.JPEG` sits in the hero, small and quiet next to the text.
- **About**: two or three plain first-person sentences. Education stays in the resume timeline so nothing is duplicated.
- **Old identity**: "Data Scientist / Software Engineer", the typing animation and data-science items (e.g. ASEAN Data Scientist Explorers finalist) leave the hero and About; they may survive only as a resume or "More work" line.
