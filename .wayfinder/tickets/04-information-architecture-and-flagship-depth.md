## Question

What sections appear, in what order, and how deep do flagship projects go: in-page expand, dedicated detail pages, or links out? What is the shape of the compact 'More work' list, and where does the resume timeline sit? Consider the survey of conventions and the collected evidence for the flagships.

## Resolution

Single-column, proof-first page. Sections in order: **Hero** (identity, scope line, headshot, About text folded in, email CTA) -> **Flagship projects** -> **Experience** (compact resume timeline) -> **More work** -> **Contact**.

- **Navigation**: minimal sticky top bar with about four anchor links plus the light/dark toggle.
- **Flagship depth**: card only, no detail pages, no in-page expand. Each card links out to its GitHub repo. A dedicated Local Notebook page can be a later effort, not part of this spec.
- **Card contents**: image, title, one-line problem/approach/result, 1-2 hard results (or a qualitative line where none exist), stack tags, repo link. The ICCSCI paper card carries a venue credit line.
- **Missing evidence**: accept a qualitative line for the RAG chatbot and From-Scratch rather than invent numbers or block; missing numbers, GIFs, paper DOI and image licences are owner follow-ups for the implementation session.
- **Flagship order**: Local Notebook, skin-disease paper (ICCSCI), Medical RAG chatbot, From-Scratch.
- **More work**: plain list, one line per item (name, one-clause description, GitHub link), sorted by relevance to AI work; old coursework and Tableau/Canva analyses dropped or reduced to a line.
- **Reserved slots**: the timeline is the Experience section after the flagships; private-repo/thesis write-ups sit in More work as no-code-link lines or under the timeline, final placement decided in the write-ups ticket.
